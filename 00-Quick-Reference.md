# Quick Reference

Simple prompts for daily learning interactions. Click subject headers for full details.

## [Mathematics](01-Mathematics.md)

| Category | Prompt | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Kitchen Chemist** | "This recipe feeds 3. We have 8 guests. Go get the spoons and show me the new ratios." | Cooking/Dinner |
| **Change Crisis** | "I paid with ₹500 for a ₹32 item. You have no ₹100s. Pay me back from this pile." | Market Play |
| **Traffic Jam** | "Look at the road. Are there more wheels or windows right now? Don't count, estimate." | Travel |
| **Lego Shadow** | "I built a shape. I will describe it. You build the copy without looking." | Play |
| **Lazy Math** | "The '4' button is broken. How do we calculate 24 x 6?" | Homework Help |
