# Mathematics

*Based on "Learning How to Learn" principles.*

**The Goal:** Move from Abstract sums to Physical & Intuitive reasoning.

---

## 🌽 Mode 1: The Kitchen Chemist (Ratios & Fractions)
*Target: Proportional reasoning and real-world measurement.*

**The "Recipe Disaster" (Scaling)**
"This recipe for Maggi/Dal/Cake feeds 3 people. Tonight, 5 cousins are coming suitable guests (Total 8). Go get the ingredients/spoons."
*   *Prompt:* "How much water do we need exactly? Use the real cups to show me. Is it double? Less than double?"

**The "Rotten Apple" (Percentages)**
*Go to the fruit bowl/fridge.*
*   *Prompt:* "If 2 of these 10 tomatoes were rotten, what percentage is lost? What if we bought 50 tomatoes and the 'rot rate' was the same?"

**The "Perfect Pour" (Volume)**
"Here is a 1-liter bottle and a small glass (approx 200ml). Estimate how many glasses fill the bottle. Now pour and check. Were you under or over? Why?"

---

## 💰 Mode 2: The Kirana Tycoon (Money & Constraints)
*Target: Mental math under pressure and making change.*

**The "Change Crisis"**
*Dump a pile of coins and notes on the table.*
*   *Setup:* "You are the shopkeeper. I am a rude customer."
*   *Prompt:* "I bought milk for ₹32. I gave you a ₹500 note. You have NO ₹100 notes / NO ₹50 notes. Pay me back using this pile. Hurry, there is a line!"

**The "Budget Birthday"**
"You have exactly ₹2000 for a party. You need: Cake (₹500), Chips (₹20/pack), and Return Gifts (₹50/each). How many friends can you invite?
*   *Twist:* "Oh no! The Cake price went up to ₹700. Who do you un-invite, or what do you cut?"

**The "Best Deal" (Unit Rates)**
*Show two items on Amazon or a grocery flyer.*
*   *Prompt:* "1kg of Surf Excel is ₹150. A 4kg pack is ₹550. Which is actually cheaper per kg? Is the big pack always a deal?"

---

## 🚗 Mode 3: The Fermi Guesser (Estimation)
*Target: Order-of-magnitude thinking and logic.*

**The "Traffic Jam"**
*Look out the window at the road.*
*   *Prompt:* "How many *wheels* are on that road right now? Don't count. Guess. Are there more wheels or windows?"
*   *Reasoning:* (Cars = 4, Autos = 3, Bikes = 2).

**The "Crowd Counter"**
"Imagine a cricket stadium (like Wankhede/Chinnaswamy). If every person buys one samosa, how many trucks of potatoes do we need?"
*   *Steps:* Stadium capacity -> Samosas -> Potatoes per samosa -> Potatoes per truck.

**The "Hair" Question**
"Do you have more hair on your head, or does the dog/cat have more hair on its body?"

---

## 🧱 Mode 4: The Blind Architect (Spatial Reasoning)
*Target: Vocabulary of shape and visualization.*

**The "Lego Shadow"**
*Build a weird shape with 5-10 Lego/blocks behind a book.*
*   *Prompt:* "I have built a shape. I will describe it. You build the exact same copy without looking. 'Put a red 2x4 on top of the blue 4x4, but offset to the left...'"

**The "Wrapper Reverse"**
*Take a chocolate wrapper or box.*
*   *Prompt:* "If I flatten this toothpaste box, what shape will it be? Draw the 'net' (flat pattern) before I crush it."

---

## 🧮 Mode 5: The "Lazy" Mathematician (Patterns & Logic)
*Target: Finding shortcuts (Algorithmic thinking).*

**The "Broken Calculator"**
"The '4' button on this calculator is broken. How do I calculate 24 x 6 using the other keys?"
*   *Solutions:* (20+4)x6? No 4 allowed. (23+1)x6? (12x2)x6?

**The "Gauss Sum"**
"I want to add the numbers 1 to 20. I am too lazy to do 1+2+3... Is there a cheat code? (Hint: Pair the first and last)."

**The "Calendar Hack"**
"If today is date X and day Y, what day will it be on the same date next month? Is there a pattern?"
