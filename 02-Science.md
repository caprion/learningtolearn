# Science

*Based on "Learning How to Learn" principles.*

**The Goal:** Move from Textbook Answers to Observation & Testing.

---

## 🔬 Mode 1: The Why Chain (Deep Processing)
*Target: Moving past surface-level facts (Feynman Technique).*

**The "5 Whys"**
"Pick a simple fact. Ask 'Why' 5 times until we hit the bottom of our knowledge."
*   *Example:*
    1.  Why involves the sky blue? (Scattering)
    2.  Why does it scatter? (Atmosphere)
    3.  Why do we have an atmosphere? (Gravity)...
    4.  *Goal:* It's okay to say "I don't know" and Google the last step.

**The "Alien Visitor"**
"Explain meaningful simple (like a Toaster or a Tree) to an alien who has never seen it. You cannot use words like 'electricity' unless you explain them."

---

## 🍳 Mode 2: Kitchen Physics (States of Matter & Heat)
*Target: Tangible understanding of abstract concepts.*

**The "Ghee/Ice Cream" Experiment**
"Look at the Ghee or Coconut Oil. Is it solid or liquid today? Why?"
*   *Hypothesis:* "If we put it in the sun for 5 mins, what happens? What if we put it in the fridge?" (Melting points).

**The "Sweaty Glass"**
"Put cold water in a steel glass. Wait 2 minutes. Why is the *outside* wet? Did the water leak through the steel?"
*   *Concept:* Condensation (Water vapor in the air).

**The "Dissolve Race"**
"Boring water vs. Hot water. Sugar vs. Salt. Which dissolves fastest? Make a bet (Hypothesis) before we stir."

---

## 🌿 Mode 3: The Botany Safari (Biology & Adaptation)
*Target: Observation skills.*

**The "Leaf Detective"**
"Go outside/balcony. Bring me 3 *different* leaves."
*   *Question:* "Why is this one big and floppy (Banana/Money plant) and this one tiny/spiky (Neem/Rose)? What is the plant trying to do?" (Sunlight capture vs. Water saving).

**The "Ants Line"**
"Find an ant line. Disrupt it gently (put a leaf in the way). What do they do? How do they know where to go? (Pheromones/Smell)."

---

## ⚡ Mode 4: The Broken Machine (Mechanics)
*Target: System thinking.*

**The "Fan/Cycle" Study**
"Look at the ceiling fan. How does it spin? Where is the motor?"
"Look at your bicycle. If I took off the chain, would the pedals work? Why?"

**The "Light Switch" Trace**
"When you press this switch, the light turns on. Trace the invisible wire with your finger. Where does it go? To the wall? To the main board? To the power station?"
